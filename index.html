Game Rules menu_book

Build a valid sequence using DFA rules:

1. Start with START and end with STOP.

2. Include at least one movement command (FORWARD or BACKWARD).

3. Do PICK before DROP, and do not pick twice without dropping.

4. Complete at least one full pick-drop task before STOP.

5. No more than 2 consecutive turns (LEFT/RIGHT).

6. Movement uses 1 energy; RECHARGE resets energy to 3.

7. No immediate reverse movement (FORWARD right after BACKWARD, or vice versa).

8. Must include at least one counter-clockwise loop: FORWARD, LEFT repeated 4 times.
